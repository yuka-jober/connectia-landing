Questions, answered.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. If you can fill in a profile, you can build your page. Most people go live in under 5 minutes.

What does the AI learn from?+

Three things: (1) the one source you give it — your website URL or intro doc, (2) everything you put on your page itself — services, posts, videos, and (3) optional imports like Instagram or YouTube. It only answers from what you've provided.

What if the AI doesn't know an answer?+

It won't make things up. It collects the visitor's question and contact info, and forwards it straight to you.

Do visitors need to log in or install anything?+

No. Your page opens in any browser. Visitors just start chatting — no login, no app, no forms.

Can it speak other languages?+

Yes. Your AI automatically replies in whatever language your visitor uses.

Where do my leads go?+

Straight to your dashboard and email — name, contact, and a summary of what they asked for.
